# Garage

Ideas here are not implemented commitments.

## Collaboration

- Message threads and thread-specific unread state.
- Custom organization roles and granular permissions.
- Bots, webhooks, workflow automation, and scheduled messages.
- Organization analytics and moderation/audit history.

## Native and Offline

- iOS runtime acceptance and Sign in with Apple planning.
- CallKit, Android Telecom, full-screen/lock-screen call actions, and stronger offline call wake behavior.
- Persistent encrypted mobile cache, queued sends, and background synchronization.

## Media and Intelligence

- Recording/transcription with explicit consent and retention controls.
- SIP/phone integration, E2EE, and device-audio screen sharing.
- Group direct calls, multiple concurrent media sessions, and per-contact ringtones.
- Persistent Echo history only after a separate privacy/data-retention design.

## Operations

- Automated load/failure test suites and capacity baselines.
- Backup restore drills, formal SLOs, and cost budgets.
- Store submission automation after release workflow behavior is proven.
